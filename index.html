Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you handle taxes in more than one state?+
Yes. We regularly prepare and file across New York, Connecticut, and New Jersey, including multi-state returns for people who live in one and work in another.
Can you do my bookkeeping too, not just my taxes?+
Absolutely. We offer ongoing (or catch-up) bookkeeping, reconciliation, and sales-tax compliance so your business stays clean and audit-ready all year — not just at filing time.
What does it cost?+
It depends on the complexity of your return or business. We'll give you a clear, upfront quote on a free consultation — no surprises.
How does the client portal work?+
Through our secure TaxDome portal you can upload documents, e-sign your return, message us, and pay invoices from any device, 24/7. We'll set you up at your first appointment.
Are you taking new clients?+
Yes — individuals and small businesses are both welcome. Reach out by phone or email and we'll find a time that works.
